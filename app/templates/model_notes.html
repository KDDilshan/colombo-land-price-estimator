What powers the estimates

A tuned XGBoost model trained on real listing data across {{ division_count }} GN divisions in Colombo district. Inputs include plot size, land type, proximity to schools/hospitals/banks/transport, and environmental and spatial variables (elevation, surface water occurrence, height above nearest drainage, distance to stream and to the Kelani, Sentinel-1 SAR flooded fraction, and Landsat NDVI). The model uses these as associations with asking prices; it makes no causal claim about any of them.

The past flooding panel is separate from the model. It reports, for the GN division clicked, the share inside the Survey Department of Sri Lanka's flood maps for May 2016, May 2018 and Cyclone Ditwah (November 2025), and the years the Disaster Management Centre's DesInventar database (1974–2019) records flooding there. It is descriptive, not a prediction, and a GN with no record may still flood.
