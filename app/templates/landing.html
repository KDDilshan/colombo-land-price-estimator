What you get

Click-to-price

Click a point or draw a plot on the map. The division, area, and environmental profile are resolved automatically.

Past flooding, from official records

Next to every estimate: whether the GN division was inside the Survey Department's 2016, 2018 and 2025 flood maps, and the years the DMC recorded flooding there. Shown as information — not used to set the price.

Nearby amenities Pro

Schools, hospitals, supermarkets, banks, bus stops and rail stations near any plot, pulled live from OpenStreetMap.

Dashboard, history & PDF reports Pro

Every estimate is saved, summarised in charts on your dashboard, and exportable as a shareable PDF report.
