1. What we collect

  • Account info: email, name, and (if you use Google sign-in) your Google account ID. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, never in plain text.
  • Estimate history: the divisions, plot sizes, land types, and prices you look up, tied to your account, so your dashboard and PDF exports work.
  • Basic technical data: IP address (used for rate-limiting and abuse prevention), and a session cookie that keeps you logged in.
  • Billing: if you subscribe to Pro, Stripe handles and stores your payment details — we only receive your subscription status, not your card number.

2. How we use it

To provide the service (run estimates, enforce free-plan limits, show your history), to process payments via Stripe, to send account-related emails (like password resets), and to keep the service secure and working.

3. Who we share it with

  • Stripe — payment processing, if you subscribe.
  • Google — only if you choose "Sign in with Google."
  • OpenStreetMap's Overpass API — receives the coordinates you look up when viewing nearby amenities; no account information is sent.
  • Our hosting provider, to run the service. We don't sell your data.

4. Data retention

We keep account and estimate data while your account is active. Contact us to request deletion of your account and associated data.
